🎯 Marketing Cloud Discovery Quick Guide

Your visual cheat sheet for uncovering what marketers really need

⚡

30-Second Prep

  • Check their website & socials
  • Are they running paid ads? (Google, LinkedIn, Meta)
  • Subscribe to their email list
  • LinkedIn stalk the attendees
  • Note their marketing channels
  • Check industry & competitors
Pro Move: Come with 2-3 observations about their marketing. "I noticed you're running LinkedIn ads—tell me about that strategy" = instant credibility.
🔥

5 Power Questions

Memorize these. Everything else flows from them.

What metric, if you moved it, would make you a hero to leadership?
Walk me through your current marketing tech stack. What makes you want to throw your laptop?
If I gave you a magic wand for your marketing ops, what's the first thing you'd fix?
Tell me about a campaign you're really proud of. What made it work?
What would a career-defining win look like for you this year?
📊

Channels & Campaigns

📧 Email Marketing
  • What marketing automation tool are you using? And do you love it or hate it?
  • Open rates? Click rates? Better or worse than you want?
  • How sophisticated is your segmentation?
  • Can you see email journey in your CRM?
  • What email automation do you wish you could build?
💰 Paid Media
  • What channels? (Google, LinkedIn, Meta, display?)
  • What's working? What's burning budget?
  • Can you attribute spend to actual revenue?
  • How do you track performance across channels?
  • Can you retarget based on email/web behavior?
🌐 Web & Social
  • How personalized is your web experience?
  • Can you track individual customer journeys?
  • Where do prospects typically drop off?
  • How consistent is messaging across channels?
🔧

Data & Tech Stack

🗂️ Current Tools
  • What tools are you using today?
  • Which ones do you love? Which frustrate you daily?
  • Where does data get stuck between systems?
  • What tools have you tried & abandoned? Why?
Draw it out: Create a simple tech stack map during the call. Boxes and arrows showing how data flows (or doesn't). This visual helps them see their own pain.
📈 Data & Analytics
  • Do you have the adequate information about each customer to be an effective marketer?
  • Can you see unified view of each customer?
  • What questions can't you answer about customers?
  • How are you personalizing with data?
  • What insights do you need to make confident decisions?
😤 Pain Points (Go Deep!)
  • What keeps you up at night?
  • What takes 10x longer than it should?
  • What manual work is killing your team's soul?
  • What's the cost? (Time? Money? Opportunities? Morale?)
  • What happens if you don't fix this in 6 months?
👥

People & Politics

🎯 The Humans
  • What are YOU personally accountable for this year?
  • Where do you see your career heading?
  • What accomplishment would you put on your resume?
  • If this gave you back 10 hours/week, what would you do with it?
  • What would make this a career-defining win?
🏢 Decision-Making
  • Who else needs to be involved?
  • Who signs the check? (Economic Buyer)
  • Who's the internal champion?
  • Who might push back? Why?
  • What's driving the timeline?
  • What criteria matter most in evaluation?
Map it: Create a quick stakeholder map. Name, role, champion/neutral/blocker, what they care about personally.
⚔️ Competition
  • Looking at other solutions?
  • Who are your top 3 competitors doing in marketing that worries you?
  • Ever worked with Salesforce before? Experience?
